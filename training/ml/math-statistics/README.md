## Goal
Statistical EDA: analyze a numeric dataset, describe distributions, correlations, outliers and limitations.

## Study topics
1. Linear algebra: vectors, matrices, shapes, dot products and norms
2. Statistics: mean, median, variance, standard deviation, covariance and correlation
3. Probability: conditional probability, independence, Bayes theorem and distributions
4. Calculus: derivatives, partial derivatives, gradients and chain rule
5. Optimization: loss, learning rate, gradient descent and convergence
6. NumPy practice: vectorization, descriptive statistics and visualization

3. Change inputs/parameters and record observations.
4. Complete the exercises and project.
5. Document decisions, metrics, failure cases and limitations.
